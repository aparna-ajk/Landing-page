2. Sample Types & Vacutainer Specifications

Biological specimen catalog, primary container specs, preservation additives, and rejection criteria.

Whole Blood (EDTA)
Vacutainer: Lavender Cap (K2 / K3 EDTA)
  • Volume: 3.0 mL • Invert 8-10 times
  • Uses: CBC, ESR, HbA1c, Blood Grouping
  • Stability: 24h at 2-8°C • No Clots Allowed
Serum (SST Gel Separator)
Vacutainer: Gold / Yellow Cap (Silica Clot Activator)
  • Volume: 4.0 mL • Allow 30m clotting
  • Centrifuge: 3500 RPM for 10 minutes
  • Uses: LFT, KFT, Lipid Profile, Hormones, Serology
Citrate Plasma (Coagulation)
Vacutainer: Light Blue Cap (3.2% Na-Citrate)
  • Strict 9:1 blood-to-anticoagulant ratio
  • Uses: PT/INR, aPTT, D-Dimer, Fibrinogen
  • Rejection: Underfilled (<90% fill line)
Fluoride Plasma (Glycolytic)
Vacutainer: Grey Cap (Na-Fluoride + K-Oxalate)
  • Inhibits glycolysis; preserves glucose
  • Volume: 2.0 mL • Invert 8 times
  • Uses: Fasting Glucose, Oral GTT, Lactate
Lithium Heparin Plasma
Vacutainer: Green Cap (Li-Heparin)
  • Volume: 3.0 mL • Rapid plasma turnaround
  • Uses: STAT Cardiac Troponin-I, Electrolytes
  • Advantage: Zero clotting delay
Clean-Catch Urine
Container: Sterile 60mL Amber Cup
  • Mid-stream clean catch specimen
  • Uses: Routine Urinalysis, Microscopic, Culture
  • Process within 1 hour or refrigerate at 4°C
Cerebrospinal Fluid (CSF)
Container: Sterile Clear Tubes 1-4
  • Lumbar puncture collection by physician
  • Immediate hand delivery • DO NOT REFRIGERATE
  • Uses: Cell count, Protein, Glucose, Gram Stain
Swabs / VTM (Respiratory)
Container: 3mL Viral Transport Medium (VTM)
  • Synthetic Dacron swab (no calcium alginate)
  • Uses: RT-PCR Influenza, SARS-CoV-2, RSV
  • Store at 2-8°C up to 72h
